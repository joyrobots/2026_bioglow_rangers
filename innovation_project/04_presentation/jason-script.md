# Jason · Design

**Hook**

What if we could find the EAB early, before the ash trees die?

**Body**

We compared three ideas: a camera, a microphone, and a vibration sensor.

The larvae live under the bark, so a camera can't see them, and a microphone can't hear them.

But when they chew under the bark, they make tiny vibrations in the wood.

So we chose a piezoelectric sensor, which turns tiny vibrations into an electric signal.

With it, we designed ArborListen to feel what we can't see or hear.

From our sketches, we have a sensor that touches the bark, and an M5Stack that reads the signal and shows it on the screen.
