## 1. Jasper · Robot base design and Mission 11

**Robot base design**

For our robot, we are using the Rig. We picked a low base so we can reach the smaller missions more easily. A low base also keeps the centre of gravity low, and the wide base makes the robot hard to tip over.

**Mission 11**

I am doing Mission 11, Window to the Past, for 20 points.

For the attachment, we have a flat, egg-shaped passive arm to pull the root cover down. We used the egg shape because it can easily go over the ledge going forward, and going back it can pull the root cover down.

On the left side, we have an active attachment that looks like a basket to collect the seed.

## 2. Bowen · Missions 1 and 2

My missions are Mission 1, Drone Survey, for 30 points, and Mission 2, Exploding Seeds, for 30 points.

For attachments, I have a passive attachment: an arm that pushes the scan marker and lifts the drone. I also have an active attachment, like a basket, that hooks onto the seeds. It catches one seed and pushes the other two to the side.

You might notice I added a chicken guy and some pins for spare parts.

---

## 3. Jonathan · Missions 8 and 9

My missions are Mission 8, Tangled, for 30 points, and Mission 9, Research Platform, for 30 points.

For these missions we have two attachments: one to lift the research platform and catch the seed, and the other to untangle the Tangled mission.

One cool part is that since the platform is heavy, we used a worm gear to lift it. A worm gear makes the motor much stronger, and it locks, so the platform can't fall back down.

## 4. Jason · Missions 7 and 13

I have completed Mission 7, Humongous Fungus, for 20 points, and Mission 13, Keystone Species, for 30 points.

For my attachment, I have an active dumper to dump Squirmy, our mascot, into the restoration platform for Keystone Species. Then my flipper flips the mycelium.

At first I used a grabber to pull the mycelium, but I found that a flipper could flip it easily.

## On the side · Strategy (draft, not in the speaking order yet)

Our strategy is to group missions that are close together into one run, so we don't waste time driving.

For example, Flip the Rock, Lucky Leaves and Reaching Roots are in the same area, and Tangled and the Research Platform are both at the tree.

Three of our runs start from red and two start from blue, so each run starts on the side closest to its missions.
