**Body**

We don't want that to happen to more trees, so we kept testing ArborListen and making it better.

Our first version was a flat vibration sensor that sat on the bark.

But the larvae are very small and the bark is thick, so the sensor might miss them.

So for our second version, we added a needle that goes deeper into the bark, and we upgraded the sensor's sensitivity.

Then we searched for the hertz of the EAB larvae, so we can set the sensor to that exact hertz.

For our next iteration, we plan to add a waterproof cover to keep the sensor dry in the rain.

**Missions 8 and 9**

My missions are Mission 8, Tangled, for 30 points, and Mission 9, Research Platform, for 30 points.

For these missions we have two attachments: one to lift the research platform and catch the seed, and the other to untangle the Tangled mission.

One cool part is that since the platform is heavy, we used a worm gear to lift it. A worm gear makes the motor much stronger, and it locks, so the platform can't fall back down.
