# Rossy · Identify

**Hook**

Did you know that mature ash trees have been almost completely wiped out across the Greater Toronto Area?

**Body**

The Toronto and Region Conservation Authority, TRCA, has already cut down over 45,000 ash trees.

The emerald ash borer, or EAB, is an invasive beetle from Asia.

It was first found in Ontario in 2002, and it kills unprotected ash trees.

Its tiny larvae chew S-shaped tunnels under the bark that cut off the tree's food and water.

Since they are hidden under the bark and cannot be seen from the outside, we have developed ArborListen to detect these larvae early.
