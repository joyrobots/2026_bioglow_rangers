# Bowen · Create

**Hook**

How can a tiny computer feel a bug chewing inside a tree?

**Body**

ArborListen has two parts: a vibration sensor and an M5Stack.

We programmed our sensor to detect the frequency of the vibrations that EAB larvae make.

The M5Stack reads the signal, and its screen shows the tree's stats and the vibrations.

Let me demo it.

First, I press the "Ready" button.

Now watch the screen when I scratch the wood.

*(Scratch the wood. Point to the screen.)*

The screen shows the vibration!

A scratch is not a real larva, but it shows that our sensor can feel tiny vibrations in wood.
