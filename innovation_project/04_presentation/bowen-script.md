# Bowen · Create

**Hook**

How can a tiny computer feel a bug chewing inside a tree?

**Body**

ArborListen has two parts: a vibration sensor and an M5Stack.

We programmed our sensor to detect the frequency of the vibrations that EAB larvae make.

The M5Stack reads the signal, and its screen shows the tree's stats and the vibrations.

Let me demo it.

First, I press the "Ready" button.

Now watch the screen when I scratch the wood.

*(Scratch the wood. Point to the screen.)*

The screen shows the vibration!

A scratch is not a real larva, but it shows that our sensor can feel tiny vibrations in wood.

# Bowen · Robot Game

**Missions 1 and 2**

My missions are Mission 1, Drone Survey, for 30 points, and Mission 2, Exploding Seeds, for 30 points.

Starting from the red side, I complete Drone Survey first and come back home to realign. Then I drive from home to complete Exploding Seeds, and end back on red.

For attachments, I have a passive attachment: an arm that pushes the scan marker and lifts the drone. I also have an active attachment, like a basket, that hooks onto the seeds. It catches one seed and pushes the other two to the side.

You might notice I added a chicken guy and some pins for spare parts.
