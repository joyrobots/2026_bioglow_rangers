## What We Learned in Class

GitHub is our online "vault" where we save robot code, notes and ideas so everyone can see them and nothing gets lost. A **repository (repo)** is our team's project folder. A **commit** saves your work with a short note about what you changed.

## Part 1 Put Your Robot Program in the Team Vault

With a parent, upload **your SPIKE Prime mission program (`.llsp3` file)** into our team folder: **robot_game/phase1_single_mission/**.

**File name rule:** Start your file name with your **mission number + first name** so nobody overwrites anyone else's file. Example: **M3Roussy.llsp3**

## Upload Checklist

- [ ] **1. Sign in to GitHub** and go to **github.com/joyrobots/2026_bioglow_rangers**.
  Open **robot_game**, then **phase1_single_mission**.
- [ ] **2.** Click **Add file**, then **Upload files** (top right, next to the green **Code** button).
- [ ] **3. Drag your `.llsp3` file** into the box, or click **choose your files**.
- [ ] **4.** In **Commit changes**, write a short note in our team format: **[Mission][Name] what you did**.
  Example: **[M3][Roussy] left and right turn**
- [ ] **5.** Click the green **Commit changes** button.
- [ ] **6.** Go back to **phase1_single_mission**. Check that you can see your file.

**Your job:** Design how an **M5Stack and a sensor** could "listen" to the tree and catch the larvae early!
