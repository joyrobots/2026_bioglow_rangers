# Week 4 Class Plan — BioGlow Rangers

**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Focus:** Tree-tour reflection, teamwork, and Tree Stethoscope design

**Materials:** Tour photos/notes, paper, pencils or a whiteboard and markers, Pictionary word slips, a timer, LEGO or prototyping supplies, M5Stack devices, USB cables, and computers ready to program.

## Timeline

| Time | Activity | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Opening riddle | “I have rings but no fingers, a trunk but no suitcase, and branches but no hands.” Answer: a tree. |
| 0:05–0:30 | Tree tour and existing solutions | Share observations (10 min); explore EAB traps, tree-injection treatments, then human monitoring (10 min); record questions and possible design ideas (5 min). |
| 0:30–0:45 | Teamwork: Pictionary | Draw tree, nature, and robotics clues while teammates guess. Rotate artists. Instructions below. |
| 0:45–0:55 | M5Stack “Hello World” | Together, display “Hello, Rangers!”, change the greeting, and save the program. |
| 0:55–1:00 | Shared planning | Sketch wood → sensor → M5Stack → screen. Agree on one simple test and each group's goal. |
| 1:00–1:25 | Split assignment | Half: sketch/build the device design. Half: extend the M5Stack program with a button that changes “Ready” to “Test signal.” |
| 1:25–1:30 | Show and save | Each group demonstrates for 2 minutes; use the final minute to save code, photograph the design, and choose the next step. |

## Pictionary — 15 minutes

- **2 min — Set up:** Make two teams and explain the rules: drawing only, with no talking, letters, or numbers from the artist.
- **10 min — Play:** Teams alternate turns. An artist secretly picks a word and has 60 seconds to draw while their team guesses. Award one point for a correct guess. Rotate artists each turn and use any remaining time for transitions.
- **3 min — Reflect:** Ask: “Which drawings helped you understand quickly? How did you build on each other's guesses? How can pictures help us explain our device?”

**Word bank:** Tree, leaf, roots, beetle, forest, robot, battery, computer, thermometer, microscope, treehouse, robot planting a tree.

**Coach tip:** Start with easy words. Encourage everyone's guesses and celebrate creative clues rather than drawing skill.

## Coach reminders

- The Rangers could not confidently identify ash trees during their search; this does not mean Markham has none.
- Compare each existing approach's purpose, strengths, and limitations; record uncertain claims as research questions.
- The button demo is a simulated input, not evidence of insect detection.
- Rotate hands-on roles so everyone contributes.

**Takeaways:** Tour notes, solution comparison, a labelled design sketch, saved M5Stack code, and one recorded test result.
